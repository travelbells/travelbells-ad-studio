# 🇨🇦 TravelBells Social Media Ad Studio & Campaign Engine

A high-converting, AI-powered social media ad generator and campaign management studio tailored for **TravelBells Immigration Inc.** (CRIC / RCIC Verified Firm).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Universal AI Copy & Bullet Generator**:
   - Type any topic (e.g. *Mobilité Francophone, Asylum Seekers, OINP, PGWP Extensions, Registered Nurses, Skilled Trades*) to dynamically produce bilingual ad copy and 4 high-impact bullet points.

2. **Multi-Format 300 DPI Vector Graphics**:
   - **Square (1:1)**: `1080x1080` for Instagram Feed & Facebook Posts.
   - **Story (9:16)**: `1080x1920` for Instagram Stories, Reels & TikTok.
   - **Landscape (16:9)**: `1200x630` for Facebook Desktop Banners & LinkedIn.

3. **100% Brand Consistency**:
   - **Large Prominent Logo Card**: High-contrast top-left TravelBells white container card.
   - **Uniform 4-Pill Crimson Footer**: Website, Email, Phone, and `📍 Ontario, Canada • Licensed RCIC Member` styled in matching `#5B1425` crimson pills across all formats.

4. **1-Click 3-in-1 ZIP Bundle Download**:
   - Generates and downloads `travelbells_3in1_creatives_bundle.zip` containing all 3 vector graphic files and formatted ad copy.

5. **Direct Social Media Publisher & Webhook Dispatcher**:
   - Publish directly to Instagram & Facebook or dispatch payloads to Make.com / Meta API.
